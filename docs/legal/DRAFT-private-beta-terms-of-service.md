## 2. Accounts

You may register with full name, email, password, and optional phone number. Passwords must be at least eight characters. Email and password are verified on Reelyou’s servers when the Reelyou backend is enabled for your build.

Keep your credentials confidential. You are responsible for activity under your account.

**Account deletion.** You may request deletion in **Settings → Delete account** (when signed in with the Reelyou backend) or by emailing **reelyou.support@gmail.com**. After a request, your account is hidden from others immediately. You may cancel the request within **30 days** in the app when the backend is enabled. Reelyou schedules purge of applicable data from **active systems within 90 days** after the request. Copies may remain in backups or provider systems for additional time where applicable (see Privacy Policy).

## 3. Your content

You may create Skywrites and attach media subject to app rules and visibility settings. When the backend is configured, shared content and media may be stored on Reelyou-operated systems (application database and object storage).

Much of the app also stores data on your device; local data may remain even when you use the server.

**Visibility.** You control who can see content using in-app visibility and My Sky privacy settings (for example private, connections/orbit, sky friends, and public where offered).

**Ownership.** You retain ownership of content you submit.

**License (draft — counsel review required).** You grant Reelyou a **non-exclusive** license to host, store, reproduce, display, and perform your content **only as needed to operate Reelyou**, including delivery according to your visibility choices and enforcement of deletion, recovery, and account-deletion rules. This license ends when your content is deleted or purged from active systems, except for limited copies retained as described in the Privacy Policy. Reelyou does not claim ownership of your content.

**Deletion and recovery (server-backed content).** When you delete a server-backed Skywrite, it becomes **inaccessible to others immediately**. You may **recover** it within **30 days** using Reelyou’s API (recoverable list and recover action when the backend is enabled). After that window, Reelyou **purges** the post and associated media from active systems on a scheduled basis.

**Skyreel and saved Skywrites.** Saved Skywrites remain until you delete them or your account is purged. **Skyreel** is a **24-hour** visibility window for recent Skywrites (timed on Reelyou’s servers when the backend is enabled, and on your device otherwise); when it ends, the saved Skywrite remains in your library unless you delete it.

## 5. Social features and discovery

You may follow, block, and manage social connections where the feature is enabled.

**Contact-based friend discovery (optional, separate from Terms acceptance).** If you choose to match contacts, the app sends phone numbers and email addresses from the contacts you allow to Reelyou’s servers over HTTPS (up to 150 identifiers per request). The server normalizes those values and compares them to one-way blind indexes of other users who opted in to be discoverable. Reelyou does not use this feature to store your address book as a contact list for unrelated purposes. Discoverability is off by default until you change it in settings.

**Privacy controls.** You can adjust discovery settings, My Sky visibility, and blocked or limited users in settings.

## 6. Moderation and reporting

You may report profiles, Skywrites, and some community content through in-app flows. When the Reelyou backend is enabled, reports are **stored on Reelyou’s servers** for operator review. Reporter identity and report details are **not shown to the reported user** through the app. Reelyou **does not guarantee** that every report will be reviewed, acted on, or resolved within a particular time. There is **no dedicated moderation staff** promised for this beta.

You may also email **reelyou.support@gmail.com** with reporting or safety concerns. Submitting a report does not by itself mean an operator has reviewed it.

## 7. AI and recommendations

When configured, Reelyou’s server may call the **OpenAI API** (`/v1/chat/completions`) to generate short explanatory text for **Starpath** from structured JSON you or the app send (verified facts, reason codes, and an optional focus snippet). Reelyou does **not** store those requests in its application database after responding. OpenAI’s API policies (including default **non-training** on API business data and **abuse-monitoring retention**, typically up to 30 days unless Zero Data Retention applies) govern OpenAI’s handling—see OpenAI’s documentation and your API account settings.

The app may also use **on-device** personalization based on your settings and activity. AI and suggestions are informational, not professional advice.

Public resource links (for example feeds, arXiv, or grants listings when enabled) come from third-party sources; verify details at the original source.
